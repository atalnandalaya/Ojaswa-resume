About

A commerce scholar at the active crossroads of law, digital business, and enterprise — dedicated to disciplined execution, wide-ranging curiosity, and hands-on operational leadership.

Rooted in Agar-Malwa, Madhya Pradesh, my academic trajectory combines rigorous institutional legal training with modern digital commerce. After completing CBSE Class XII in Commerce with 82%, I embarked on two simultaneous degree programmes: the five-year integrated BA LL.B. (Hons.) at Nehru PG College, affiliated with Vikram University, Agar, alongside the prestigious online BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB).

My worldview is shaped by active participation rather than passive observation. I am a national-level quiz winner with the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), a national competitor representing Madhya Pradesh in Atya Patya, and a state bronze medalist in Unifight. Through residential design workshops at the National Institute of Design (NID MP) and presentations at the India International Science Festival (MANIT Bhopal), I have honed a multidisciplinary perspective.

Beyond academics, I draw clarity and purpose from travel, photography, and spiritual exploration. From the roaring waters of the Bhagirathi at Gangotri to the serene heights of Badrinath and Diwali nights in Agar, I believe that moving through the world dispels dogma. An active mind with relentless curiosity fuels my enthusiasm for strategy games, competitive football, music, and the visual arts.

Growing up immersed in family enterprises across education management, real estate development, and legal practice, I gained direct, early exposure to ground realities — negotiating contracts, understanding statutory compliance, and leading human teams with integrity.

The mountains call,
and I must go.

Gangotri & Badrinath · Uttarakhand · 3,400+ m

Standing before towering Himalayan massifs, where cold alpine winds sweep through ancient passes and glacial rivers carve through solid granite — this is where the noise of the world recedes. The mountains do not offer answers; they quiet the mind so you can finally ask the right questions.
